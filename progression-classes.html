Progression Classes

Finished Bronze and not ready to stop? Progression Classes are for teams who want to keep their scentwork going, building on what your dog already knows without having to sign up for the next level straight away.

Each class brings new challenges, with harder hides and bigger areas, so your dog keeps learning and keeps enjoying it. It is also a good place to sharpen things up before or after Silver.

The Detail

What You Will Work On

  • New hides and new places, so searches never go stale
  • Bigger areas and more complex searches
  • The six search types from Bronze, taken further
  • Plenty of working time in a small class
  • Help with anything specific you want to improve
Who It Suits

Is It Right For Your Dog?

Progression Classes are for teams who have finished Bronze UK Sniffer Dogs. They suit handlers who want to keep the habit going week to week, and dogs who just love the work. Reactive dogs who managed Bronze are welcome.
