Introduction Workshop

The introduction workshop is the easiest way to find out what scentwork is and whether your dog will love it. It is a single 90 minute session with no commitment past the one evening.

Most people come along not knowing what to expect, and go home amazed at how good their dog already is. Dogs use their noses to learn about the world all day long. In the workshop we show you how to point that at one job, and how to tell when your dog has found what they were looking for.

The Detail

What Happens On The Night

  • A short introduction to how scentwork works and why dogs find it so satisfying
  • Your dog makes their first finds, with plenty of rewards
  • You learn what to watch for in your dog as they search
  • Time to ask Lucy anything, including whether scentwork suits a reactive or nervous dog
  • Information on the Bronze course if you want to carry on
Who It Suits

Is It Right For Your Dog?

The workshop suits any dog that can sniff, which is all of them. We have worked with dogs from six months to thirteen years old, and with dogs who are reactive, nervous, recovering from injury or simply full of beans. No training background is needed, for you or your dog.
