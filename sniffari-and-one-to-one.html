Sniffari And One To One

Not every dog wants to learn in a group, and not every handler has a free Tuesday evening. Our Sniffari and one to one sessions run privately at SweetPea's HQ in Tewkesbury, arranged around times that suit you.

It is just you, your dog and Lucy. You can also split a session with a friend. It is the right route if your dog struggles around other dogs, or if there is one specific thing you want to crack.

The Detail

Good For

  • Dogs who find a group too much, at least to start with
  • Handlers who want a taster before joining a course
  • Working on one specific problem in a search
  • Sharing a session with a friend and their dog
  • Fitting scentwork around a busy week
Who It Suits

Is It Right For Your Dog?

One to one sessions suit any dog, and they are especially good for reactive or nervous dogs who need to build confidence before working near others. Lucy is a registered veterinary nurse as well as a UK Sniffer Dogs instructor, so you are in good hands if your dog has health or behaviour needs.
