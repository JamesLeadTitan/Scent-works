Nose First

Does Your Dog Need Hours To Wear Out?

Always on the go. Wanting to play the second you sit down. Chewing the skirting board because there is nothing better to do. That is not a badly behaved dog, it is usually a bored one, and twenty minutes of searching will settle them more thoroughly than another lap of the park.

Scentwork taps into what dogs are already brilliant at. We teach them to find one specific scent and tell you when they have found it, and the rest follows from there.

  • Builds confidence through problem solving
  • Real mental work, not just physical
  • Light exercise, kind to joints
  • Good for dogs on restricted exercise
  • Any breed, any age, any ability
  • Teaches you to read your own dog
Sniffing releases dopamine, which is why it calms dogs down rather than winding them up. Our youngest handler on a course right now is six months old and our oldest is thirteen, and we have had a nearly deaf dog work through it too.
