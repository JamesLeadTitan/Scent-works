The Foundation

Bronze UK Sniffer Dogs

The Bronze UK Sniffer Dogs course is where most of our teams start their scentwork journey. It runs over six weekly one hour lessons at Coombe Hill Farm Shop, and it is suitable for anyone wanting to get started, with no experience needed.

Classes are capped at four dogs, so every dog gets proper working time in every lesson. Lucy is a Bronze and Silver qualified UK Sniffer Dogs instructor, and you get handouts and homework through the course so the work carries on at home between lessons.

The Detail

How The Six Weeks Work

  • Weeks 1 to 3The basics. Your dog learns the target scent, how to search for it and how to tell you when they have found it.
  • Weeks 4 and 5Where the fun really starts. Novel searches in places your dog has never been, plus vehicle searches.
  • Week 6It steps up again, putting everything together for the end of the course.
Who It Suits

Is It Right For Your Dog?

We have had a wide variety of breeds through the course and are yet to find one that does not enjoy it. Our dogs range from six months to thirteen years old, and reactive dogs are welcome. Either one or two handlers can attend with each dog, and two is genuinely useful in weeks four and five, when we start blind searches and one of you needs to not know where the hide is.
