Your Group

Private Group Bookings

Got a group of friends, a walking group or a club who all fancy having a go at scentwork? We run private group sessions on request, arranged around dates that suit you.

It is a brilliant way to try something new with people you already know, and it makes a great club night or social. Lucy plans the session around your group, whether everyone is brand new or some of you have done scentwork before.

The Detail

Ideas For Your Group

  • A taster session for a dog walking group
  • A club night for a training or breed club
  • A get together for friends and their dogs
  • A refresher for teams who trained together
  • Something for a fundraiser or community group
Who It Suits

Is It Right For Your Dog?

Group sessions work for any mix of breeds and ages. If anyone in your group has a reactive or nervous dog, tell us when you get in touch and we will plan the session so everyone can take part comfortably.
