Silver UK Sniffer Dogs

Silver UK Sniffer Dogs is the next step for teams who have finished their Bronze course. It runs over four weekly one hour lessons at Coombe Hill Farm Shop, taught by Lucy, who is qualified to instruct at both Bronze and Silver level.

By now your dog knows the job. Silver is about making it harder in the right ways, so your dog has to work things out for themselves, and you get better at reading what they are telling you.

The Detail

What Changes At Silver

  • Harder hides that take real problem solving
  • Bigger search areas to cover properly
  • More distraction, so your dog learns to stay on the job
  • More advanced handling to go with all of it
  • Small classes, so you still get plenty of working time
Who It Suits

Is It Right For Your Dog?

Silver is for teams who have completed Bronze UK Sniffer Dogs, with us or elsewhere. If you trained somewhere else, message us before booking and we will make sure it is the right level for you. Reactive dogs who managed Bronze are just as welcome here.
